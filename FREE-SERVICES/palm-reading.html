Palm Reading & Palmistry

Learn about the major lines, mounts and traditional symbols used in palmistry. Explore their traditional interpretations and understand the basics of palm reading.

Understanding the Palm

Traditional palmistry studies the lines, mounts, shapes and markings of the hand.

✋
1
Heart Line

Traditionally associated with emotions, affection and relationships.

2
Head Line

Traditionally associated with thinking, intellect and mental style.

3
Life Line

Traditionally interpreted in relation to vitality, major transitions and life patterns.

4
Fate Line

Traditionally associated with career, direction and life circumstances.

Major Palm Lines

Explore the traditional meaning associated with each major palm line.

❤️

Heart Line

Usually examined near the upper portion of the palm. Traditional palmistry associates it with emotional expression, affection and relationships.

🧠

Head Line

Traditionally associated with thinking style, reasoning, communication and intellectual tendencies.

🌿

Life Line

Traditionally interpreted in relation to vitality, major life changes and personal development. It is not a literal lifespan indicator.

🧭

Fate Line

Traditionally associated with career, direction, responsibilities and changing circumstances.

🌞

Sun Line

Traditionally associated with recognition, creativity, achievement and visibility.

🌙

Mercury Line

Sometimes called the Health Line in traditional palmistry and associated with communication and practical abilities.

Common Palm Symbols

Traditional palmistry gives symbolic significance to various markings.

★
Star Traditionally linked with notable energy or significant events.
△
Triangle Traditionally associated with focused energy or ability.
○
Circle A comparatively uncommon marking with interpretations varying by location.
✚
Cross Traditionally interpreted differently depending on the line and mount.

Mounts of the Palm

Mounts are raised areas of the palm traditionally associated with planetary symbolism.

☀️
Mount of Sun

Traditionally associated with creativity, confidence and recognition.

🌙
Mount of Moon

Traditionally associated with imagination, intuition and sensitivity.

☿
Mount of Mercury

Traditionally associated with communication, wit and practical intelligence.

♀
Mount of Venus

Traditionally associated with affection, vitality and social warmth.

♂
Mount of Mars

Traditionally associated with courage, resilience and assertiveness.

♃
Mount of Jupiter

Traditionally associated with ambition, leadership and confidence.

Want a Personal Palm Reading? ✋

A traditional palm reading can consider multiple lines, mounts and markings together rather than interpreting one line in isolation.

ℹ️ Important Note

Palmistry is a traditional divination practice and its interpretations are not scientifically established predictions. A palm line should not be treated as a guaranteed prediction of health, lifespan, wealth or future events.
