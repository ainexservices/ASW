What Is Kaal Sarp Dosha?

Kaal Sarp Dosha is a traditional Jyotish concept associated with the relationship between the lunar nodes Rahu and Ketu and the remaining planets.

☊
Rahu

The ascending lunar node, traditionally associated with desire, ambition and unconventional themes.

☋
Ketu

The descending lunar node, traditionally associated with detachment, introspection and spiritual themes.

🔮
Full Chart

A complete planetary chart is needed to evaluate node-axis conditions and other modifying factors.

ℹ️ Important Calculation Note

This page is a frontend demonstration. A production engine should calculate precise sidereal longitudes for the Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn, together with Rahu/Ketu, and apply the exact selected Jyotish tradition's definition. Different traditions can differ on whether and how this Dosha is recognized, so it should not be treated as a standalone prediction.
