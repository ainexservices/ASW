How FLAMES Works

FLAMES is a classic recreational name game. The algorithm used here follows the common elimination approach.

01
Compare Names

The names are normalized and common letters are cancelled from both names.

02
Count Remaining

The letters left after cancellation are counted.

03
Eliminate

The count is used repeatedly around F-L-A-M-E-S until one letter remains.

ℹ️ Just for Fun

FLAMES is a recreational name game and has no scientific or predictive validity. Its result should be treated purely as entertainment and should not be used to make decisions about relationships or marriage.
