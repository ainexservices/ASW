Traditional Mangal Dosha Rules

Different Jyotish traditions use different reference points and cancellation rules.

1
Mars Placement

A common rule considers Mars in specific houses from the Ascendant, Moon or Venus.

2
House-Based Assessment

Commonly referenced houses include the 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th and 12th, although traditions can differ.

3
Cancellation Factors

Sign dignity, planetary aspects, conjunctions and other chart conditions can modify or cancel a Dosha indication.

4
Compatibility Analysis

A Manglik indication should not be used alone to judge marriage compatibility. Full Kundli matching provides broader context.

ℹ️ Important Calculation Note

This page is a frontend demonstration. A production engine should calculate the exact sidereal planetary positions from an astronomical ephemeris, determine the Ascendant and Moon/Venus reference houses, and then apply the selected Jyotish tradition's Dosha and cancellation rules.
