What Is Sade Sati?

Sade Sati is a traditional Jyotish concept involving Saturn's transit through three zodiac signs relative to the natal Moon sign.

1

First Phase

Saturn transits the sign before the natal Moon sign.

2

Second Phase

Saturn transits the natal Moon sign itself.

3

Third Phase

Saturn transits the sign after the natal Moon sign.

ℹ️ Important Calculation Note

This frontend version provides a demonstration result. A production Sade Sati engine should calculate the natal Moon's exact sidereal longitude, current Saturn sidereal longitude, transit dates, retrograde periods and the three phase boundaries using an astronomical ephemeris.
