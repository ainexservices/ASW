What Is Darakaraka?

In Jaimini astrology, Darakaraka is traditionally associated with spouse and partnership significations. Its interpretation depends on the complete horoscope rather than the planet alone.

💍
Lowest Degree

Among the applicable planets, the one with the lowest degree is traditionally selected as Darakaraka.
