How the Friendship Calculator Works

This tool uses name numerology as a lightweight compatibility exercise. Different numerology traditions may use different letter mappings.

🔤
Name Conversion

Alphabetic characters are converted into numerical values using the selected system.

🔢
Root Numbers

The name totals are reduced to single-digit root numbers for comparison.

🤝
Friendship Score

The two root numbers are compared to create a simple traditional compatibility score.

ℹ️ Important Note

Numerology-based friendship scores are traditional or entertainment-oriented interpretations, not scientifically validated measurements. A friendship cannot be accurately predicted by names or numbers alone.
