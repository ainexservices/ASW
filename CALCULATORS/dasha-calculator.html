Vimshottari Dasha Lords

The traditional Vimshottari system uses nine planetary periods totaling 120 years.

☀️
Sun — 6 Years

Traditionally associated with authority, identity, confidence and leadership.

🌙
Moon — 10 Years

Traditionally associated with emotions, mind, nurturing and adaptability.

🔥
Mars — 7 Years

Traditionally associated with energy, courage, competition and action.

☊
Rahu — 18 Years

Traditionally associated with ambition, unconventional experiences and desire.

🪐
Jupiter — 16 Years

Traditionally associated with learning, expansion, wisdom and opportunity.

♀
Saturn — 19 Years

Traditionally associated with discipline, responsibility and persistence.

☿
Mercury — 17 Years

Traditionally associated with communication, analysis and learning.

☋
Ketu — 7 Years

Traditionally associated with detachment, introspection and spiritual themes.

♀
Venus — 20 Years

Traditionally associated with relationships, creativity, comforts and aesthetics.

ℹ️ Important Calculation Note

This page is a frontend demonstration. A production Dasha engine should determine the Moon's exact sidereal longitude and birth Nakshatra using an astronomical ephemeris, calculate the remaining portion of the starting Nakshatra lord's period, and then derive Maha Dasha and Antardasha dates.
