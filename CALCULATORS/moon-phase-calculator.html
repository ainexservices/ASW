8 Major Moon Phases

The lunar cycle is commonly described through eight recognizable phases.

🌑
New Moon

The Moon is near the beginning of a new synodic cycle.

🌒
Waxing Crescent

A growing illuminated portion appears after the New Moon.

🌓
First Quarter

Approximately half of the visible lunar disk is illuminated.

🌔
Waxing Gibbous

More than half of the visible disk is illuminated and increasing.

🌕
Full Moon

The visible lunar disk is approximately fully illuminated.

🌖
Waning Gibbous

Illumination begins decreasing after the Full Moon.

🌗
Last Quarter

Approximately half of the visible disk remains illuminated.

🌘
Waning Crescent

The illuminated portion continues decreasing toward New Moon.

ℹ️ Calculation Note

This frontend calculator uses an approximate synodic-month algorithm for the displayed phase. A production astronomical implementation should use precise ephemeris data and the user's exact date, time, timezone and observing location when calculating Moon longitude, illumination and rise/set information.
