What is D9?

Navamsa is the ninth divisional chart in Vedic astrology. Traditionally, astrologers use it alongside the D1 chart when assessing marriage, partnerships, dharma and planetary strength.

💍
Marriage & Partnership

D9 is traditionally considered an important divisional chart for relationship and marriage analysis.

🪐
Planetary Strength

Astrologers may compare D1 and D9 placements when evaluating planetary strength and expression.
