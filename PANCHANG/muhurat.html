Muhurat Guidelines

General traditional considerations before selecting an auspicious time.

✅ Traditionally Considered

  • Tithi and Nakshatra
  • Vara and Yoga
  • Lagna and planetary positions
  • Rahu Kaal and other periods
  • Location-specific sunrise and sunset

⚠️ Check Before Finalizing

  • Exact location
  • Date and local timezone
  • Regional Panchang tradition
  • Personal Kundli compatibility
  • Event-specific requirements
